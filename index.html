04 About the mechanism

Coverage first.
Space tells us why.

CareSpace turns a changing food network into a decision: what can move now, where it can land, and whether the next investment should be food or storage.

01 · ATOMIZE

Count whole boxes

One ration is an indivisible unit with one destination, so the allocation stays honest.

02 · DIMENSIONALIZE

Measure every wall

Dry, cold, pallet, vehicle, and other capacities can bind at the same time.

03 · NET

Subtract current state

Free space and residual need both start from what is already on hand today.

04 · FILL & DIAGNOSE

Raise the waterline

Max-min coverage reveals whether a site needs food, storage, or no further action.

Operational rule: a capacity-bound site needs shelving or cold space, not another delivery. The network is re-solved as inventory moves.
