CareSpace allocation framework · POC

The Indivisible Box.

Allocating goods when space, not stock, is the wall. CareSpace uses a whole-ration unit to decide where food can land, how fairly to distribute a wave, and whether the next intervention should be food or storage.

The reframe

Coverage first.
Space tells us why.

Utilization is the medium, not the objective. A box parked in a full freezer feeds nobody, so the model maximizes people covered while treating real capacity as a hard limit. The dashboard turns that limit into a decision instead of hiding it inside a utilization percentage.

Framework · seven steps

In order, because
the order is load-bearing.

Each step removes a source of over-shipping or false capacity before the next step begins.

01 · ATOMIZE

Name the unit

One person’s ration is one indivisible box with one destination.

02 · DIMENSIONALIZE

Count every wall

Dry shelf, freezer, pallet, van, and staff capacity can bind together.

03 · REDUCE

Take the minimum

A site’s box capacity is the smallest whole-box capacity across dimensions.

04 · NET

Subtract current state

Free space and residual need begin with inventory already on hand.

05 · FILL

Raise the waterline

Spread the available wave by coverage ratio until a site hits its cap.

06 · DIAGNOSE

Read the binding wall

Supply-bound means food; capacity-bound means storage; met means stop.

07 · RE-SOLVE

Treat capacity as a rate

Re-measure as inventory leaves because yesterday’s free space decays.
